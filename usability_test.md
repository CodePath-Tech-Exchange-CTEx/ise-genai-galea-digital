* They were confused about how to interact with the feedback screen and what the emojis meant.
* The overall clarity of the feedback screen could be improved

* "How do I change it now?"

## Results

* Uncertain as to how to interact with the feedback screen
  > "Am I supposed to put the emojis in the boxes?"
  * Hypothesized Reason: All of the emoji buttons being the same color and brightness could have indicated to the user that they were to be dragged not tapped. Additionally, it is not immediately clear how to interact with the boxes in the effort section.
  * Chosen Remedy: 
    1. Visually distinguish selected vs. unselected emojis to indicate they can be tapped
    2. Add "select one" text near the 1-9 effort scale
* Ambiguous motivation scale icons
  > "I am kind of confused about what the fire represents."
  * Hypothesized Reason: The fire emoji breaks the previous pattern of emoji smiley faces.
  * Chosen Remedy: Remove the fire emoji and add a sad emoji to the beginning of the selection.
* Unclear as to how to edit calorie goal once set
  > "How do I change it now?"
  * Hypothesized Reason: There does not seem to be a clickable button to edit the calorie goal.
  * Chosen Remedy: Add an edit button next to the calorie goal that appears when it is set
